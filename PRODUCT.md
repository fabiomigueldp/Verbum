# Product

## Register

product

## Users

People who translate and refine important communication across languages, often in focused professional workflows where speed, clarity, privacy, and precise terminology matter. They may use Verbum on desktop or as an installed mobile PWA, including with touch, keyboard, reduced motion, RTL languages, or constrained connectivity.

## Product Purpose

Verbum is a local-first language tool that makes high-quality translation, refinement, and knowledge indexing feel immediate and controlled. Success means the interface disappears into the task: users can move between languages and modes without friction, retain ownership of their provider credentials and data, and trust both the linguistic output and the operational feedback.

## Brand Personality

Precise, quiet, assured. Verbum should feel like a premium physical instrument: deliberate, technically rigorous, and calm under sustained use.

## Anti-references

Generic AI chat interfaces, colorful SaaS dashboards, decorative glassmorphism, provider-centric control panels, verbose onboarding, ornamental animation, unexplained technical noise, and interfaces that trade speed or clarity for spectacle.

## Design Principles

1. The task is the interface: translation and indexing remain visually and cognitively primary.
2. Silence communicates confidence: remove anything that does not improve action, state, or understanding.
3. Immediate means trustworthy: boot, mode changes, loading, offline behavior, and recovery must feel stable and predictable.
4. Precision is visible in details: typography, spacing, touch targets, cost values, focus states, and language direction are treated as functional quality.
5. Local-first is a product promise: browser storage failures and network loss degrade gracefully without trapping the user.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.2 AA. Preserve full keyboard operation, visible focus, semantic controls, readable contrast, 44px touch targets, reduced-motion support, zoom and narrow-screen resilience, and correct RTL rendering. Never rely on color, hover, motion, or persistent browser storage as the sole carrier of meaning.
